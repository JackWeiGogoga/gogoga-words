# 《狼来了》生图提示词

以下是这次实际送入内置 ImageGen 的提示词。使用横向构图和不透明背景，中英正文由排版脚本加入。图中角色、服装和场景作为后续页的视觉参考。

## 第 4 页 · 狼真的来了

第一次生成时直接使用以下提示词。保存的输出是 [wolf-appears.png](assets/wolf-appears.png)，后续两次生图都把它作为参考图。

```text
Illustration-story. Create one horizontal 3:2 interior page illustration for an original young children's Chinese-language picture-book adaptation of the shepherd boy and the wolf. Single coherent scene. Classical Chinese rural foothill village, period-neutral folk-tale setting. This is the first scene in which a real wolf appears. At lower right a Chinese shepherd boy, age about seven, in a muted indigo crossed-collar cloth tunic with a single simple vermilion sash, dark cloth trousers and soft cloth shoes, cups his hands to call toward a tiny distant whitewashed, dark tile-roof village on the upper right; he is visibly worried, a child-sized believable gesture. Three rounded off-white sheep in the middle are startled and step away from the left edge toward the shepherd, still unharmed. At far left near an understated pine and low rock, a single alert grey-brown wolf has quietly stepped out from behind a bush, in side profile, mouth closed, small in the composition, at a clear physical distance. Shallow diagonals of a stone path and soft distant hills connect the scene; restrained late-afternoon warmth gives contrast to cool indigo, subdued jade green, ochre, and mineral vermilion. Borrow general Chinese folk woodblock-print qualities: shaped ink contours with slight hand pressure variation, limited opaque mineral color and a few printed-looking hatch marks within silhouettes, broad flat forms, restrained distant ink-wash layers, deliberate clean negative space in the sky and around all faces. The page ground is clean light warm white, no visible paper grain, no beige vignette. Sophisticated but legible for ages 4–6. Art should feel hand-printed yet gentle, with an original character silhouette and natural sheep and wolf rather than cartoon mascots. No text, no numbers, no border, no stamp, no logo, no weapons, no blood, no attack, no gaping mouth, no collage, no photorealistic or glossy 3D rendering. Preserve clean open sky in the top quarter for editable typesetting later.
```

## 第 1、2、3、5 页 · 四格素材

引用 [wolf-appears.png](assets/wolf-appears.png)，按左上、右上、左下、右下排列这四页的无字插画。成品每页只使用其中一格。

```text
Illustration-story. Generate exactly four separate landscape illustrations in ONE atlas. Use a precise 2-by-2 grid on an overall landscape 3:2 canvas, with four equal-width and equal-height cells. Divide cells with one very thin straight white vertical gutter at the true midpoint and one very thin straight white horizontal gutter at the true midpoint. Each cell is its own fully composed 3:2 landscape story page, art reaches the edges of its cell. No frames or external margins. All characters fully inside their cell; keep subjects well away from gutters. Use the supplied image only as a visual continuity reference for an original Chinese children's picture book. Match the same Chinese shepherd boy of seven with round cheek, single high hair bun tied with short vermilion ribbons, muted indigo cross-collar cloth tunic with white cuffs, vermilion sash, loose charcoal trousers, pale ankle wraps and black soft cloth shoes. Match the same three fluffy off-white sheep, the same grey-brown natural wolf, broad low dusty-jade hills, pine at left, stone path bending uphill to a small whitewashed Chinese village with grey tiled roofs. Period-neutral rural China. Keep the woodblock-inspired shaped ink contours, small carved-looking hatch marks inside shapes, muted mineral jade, indigo, ochre and vermilion, clean warm-white sky and unprinted ground, restrained distant pale washes. Children's picture-book scale, legible expressive gestures and quiet negative space. No paper grain or vignette.

Top left, story page 1: Quiet morning. The boy sits alone on a low rock near the familiar pine, head propped on one hand, bored but gentle. The three sheep graze peacefully across the lower part of the hill. The village is tiny in the upper right. No wolf and no villagers.
Top right, story page 2: First false alarm. On that same hill, the boy has just called from his rock; he is now laughing and covering his mouth. Three distinct plainly dressed Chinese villagers hurry toward him up the stone path from the right, looking worried and scanning the hillside. A woman wears a plain muted teal tunic and has a low bun, an older man wears a plain ochre jacket, and a third adult wears a brown tunic. The sheep are peaceful. No wolf.
Bottom left, story page 3: Second false alarm, almost the same framing as top right so a child notices the changed reactions. On that same stone path, the same woman in muted teal and older man in ochre have slowed down, slightly weary and skeptical; they are standing and exchanging a glance. The boy is again at his rock, gives a sheepish mischievous smile, and the same sheep graze. No wolf. The adults keep a gentle expression.
Bottom right, story page 5: After the real wolf has appeared. Show the boy from behind, small, standing at a bend in the stone path and looking uphill toward the white village. The empty stone path is a clear visual focus. No villagers at all on the path. Two or three small pale sheep have scattered across different parts of the distant slope. Slightly cooler dusk colors, quiet scene. The wolf is no longer visible. No animal injury.

No text, numbers, letters, icons, subtitles, speech bubbles, seals, layout elements or extra frames. No violence. Keep all panels in the same original print-inspired storybook style, with gentle expressions and clean sky.
```
